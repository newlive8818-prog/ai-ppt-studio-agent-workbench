# Content workflow

## Purpose

Finish what the presentation says before deciding how it looks. The first-release scenario is a reading-first sales-solution presentation for presales professionals.

## Mission first

Capture:

- maker and intended reader/listener;
- decision maker, influencer, evaluator, and possible veto role;
- desired recognition, judgment, action, and decision;
- reporting stage, time, venue, reading/presenting mode, and constraints;
- existing consensus, unresolved concern, competition, feedback, and red lines;
- available requirements, construction plans, minutes, company introductions, product materials, cases, screenshots, images, tables, and user-provided links.

If the goal is incomplete, infer a draft from the user's natural description and present a compact confirmation card. Do not start from uploaded documents alone and guess the audience or decision objective.

## Material behavior

Use one material intake stream. Users do not pre-classify inputs. AI identifies dynamic roles such as customer need, organization background, solution capability, case evidence, data, screenshot, visual reference, or narrative context. High-confidence, low-risk classification does not require item-by-item confirmation.

An incomplete case may be summarized and completed from surrounding context, but preserve three identities:

1. source fact: directly supported by a user-provided source;
2. source-derived inference: reasoned from provided context and visibly labeled;
3. AI-authored connective content: narrative, explanation, analogy, or proposal language that does not invent customer facts, numbers, results, product capabilities, or commitments.

Critical unsupported facts cannot pass G0.

## Content Blueprint

The blueprint must contain:

- title, audience, goal, narrative thesis, chapters, and transition logic;
- for every slide: `slide_id`, role, primary audience, purpose, one core claim, visible title/body/labels, complete notes or speaking prompt, cases, data, screenshot/image/diagram intent, source references, material references, proof role, relationship type, confidence, and open gaps;
- a real-material map showing which source, page, screenshot, case, and data item will appear where;
- source facts separated from inference and suggested additions;
- duplicate, contradiction, missing-evidence, and overclaim warnings.

Key slides receive full speaker wording. Ordinary slides receive concise speaking prompts. Keep both in the content blueprint and later place them in PPT speaker notes through Codex Presentations.

## Review and G0

Save the versioned `content-blueprint` artifact, run the offline content review page, and let the user review the whole narrative rather than approve every extracted item. Apply natural-language corrections as a new version. Pass G0 only when structure, per-slide claims, real materials, and source boundaries are coherent.
